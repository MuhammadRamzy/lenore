# Lenore brand

- [Brand identity (PDF)](Lenore-Brand-Identity.pdf), 50 pages:
  - A · Foundation: who we are, purpose, vision, values, positioning, audiences, promise and pillars, personality, brand architecture.
  - B · Verbal identity: messaging house, pitch and boilerplate, voice and tone, vocabulary, sample copy in English and Malayalam.
  - C · Visual identity: logo system, colour, typography, signature graphic devices, photography, layout, motion.
  - D · Touchpoints: customer journey, showroom exterior and interior, stationery, documents and stamps, packaging, uniform, vehicle, advertising, print, social, digital, gifting.
  - E · Governance: brand checklist, files and formats.
- `logo/`: master SVG logos (5 layouts × 5 colourways) and `favicon.svg`.
- `colour/`: palette as CSS, JSON and Adobe ASE swatches, with WCAG contrast.
- `elements/`: monogram patterns, oversized mark, animated logo, rubber-stamp artwork.

The full brand kit (logo PNG/PDF exports, app icons, fonts, and print-ready artwork for every touchpoint) is distributed as `Lenore-Brand-Kit.zip`; see `KIT-README.txt`. The strategy in Part A is a proposal for the owners to confirm.
