Notes
- Large formats marked "scale-1-10" are drawn at 1:10; scale up 10×.
- Replace placeholder text (Your Name, CODE-0000, ₹00,000, opening hours).
- Product photos in templates are illustrative AI-generated samples: replace
  with real photography before publishing.
- Have Malayalam copy reviewed by a native writer before print.
- Sign, vehicle and stamp artwork are design files: confirm sizes, materials
  and any permissions with the maker, and always ask for a proof.
- CMYK values are screen conversions: proof on the real paper or material.
- The mark is vector artwork redrawn from the logo used on Lenore's bills. If
  the original designer's artwork is available, compare before replacing.
- The strategy in Part A is a proposal for the owners to confirm.
